Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 20, 2026

Welcome to Isturyaheee! We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights when you visit our website or place an order for our t-shirts (Don't Be Late Tee, Everyday Comfort Tee, and Do Good Work Tee).

Information We Collect

Because Isturyaheee! operates as a lightweight static storefront, we only collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when you reach out or initiate an order. This includes:

  • Contact Inquiries: Your name, email address, and message details when you submit a query through our Contact Us form.
  • Order Details: Your name, shipping address, contact number, and selected t-shirt specifications (design, size, and quantity) when you click the Order Now button and send an order via your email client.
  • Basic Technical Data: Standard, non-identifying browser or device information automatically processed by our hosting provider (GitHub Pages) to deliver the webpage securely to your screen.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide strictly to operate our store and serve you better:

  • To process, confirm, and fulfill your t-shirt orders.
  • To respond to your questions, feedback, or customer support requests.
  • To communicate important updates regarding your order status or delivery.

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal information with third-party advertisers or data brokers.

How Our Forms and Email Ordering Work

Our website uses client-side functionality to protect your data during submission:

  • Form: Filling out our Contact Us form prepares a draft email in your device's default email application addressed to hello@examplewebsite142.com.
  • Product Orders: Clicking Order Now on a product page generates a pre-filled order email in your default email application addressed to orders@examplewebsite142.com.

Your information is only transmitted to us once you review and click "Send" inside your own email client.

Acceptable Use of the Website

When using Isturyaheee!, you agree not to:

  • Submit false, misleading, or fraudulent order requests or contact inquiries.
  • Attempt to interfere with the website's functionality, accessibility features, or hosting infrastructure.
  • Use the website or its contact channels for spam, harassment, or unlawful activities.

Cookies and Tracking

Isturyaheee! does not use invasive tracking cookies, behavioral profiling, or third-party advertising pixels. Any essential technical logs maintained by our web host are used solely for site security and performance.

Data Security and Retention

We take reasonable precautions to keep the emails and order records you send us secure and accessible only to authorized team members handling fulfillment and customer support. We retain your order and inquiry emails only for as long as necessary to complete your transaction, resolve inquiries, or comply with basic record-keeping requirements.

Your Privacy Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information you have shared with us via email.
  • Ask us to correct or update inaccurate contact or shipping details.
  • Request the deletion of your past inquiry or order emails from our inbox once your order is completed.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, please reach out to us:

  • General Inquiries: hello@examplewebsite142.com
  • Order Support: orders@examplewebsite142.com
  • Store Team: Isturyaheee!
